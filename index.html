Guide

Level 1. Full House: a unit with one empty cell. Last Digit: a digit placed 8 times has a forced 9th spot. Scanning (Cross-Hatching): the digit’s rows and columns block all but one cell of a box.

Level 2. Naked Single: a cell with one candidate. Hidden Single: a digit with only one possible cell in a unit.

Level 3. Naked Pair: two cells share the same two candidates, so those digits leave the rest of the unit. Hidden Pair: two digits fit only the same two cells, so those cells lose their other candidates.

Level 4. Pointing Pair/Triple: in a box a digit lines up in one row or column, so it leaves the rest of that line. Claiming (Box/Line Reduction): in a line a digit sits in one box, so it leaves the rest of the box.

Level 5. X-Wing: a digit has two spots in each of two lines, aligned on the same two crossing lines, so it leaves the rest of those lines.

Level 6. XY-Wing: a pivot {A,B} sees wings {A,C} and {B,C}. Cells that see both wings can’t be C.

Levels 7 and 8. Chains, colouring, uniqueness patterns and Almost Locked Sets aren’t generated here yet. The tutorial explains all 26 techniques.

How to use this page

  • Learn: pick a level. I fill in a few easy cells, show you the technique on a real cell, then mark a green square where you apply it yourself. Hint shows the relationship.
  • Play: pick a level and solve freely. Hint shows one answer at a time.
  • Notes: turn on Notes, then tap a cell and the digits to write pencil marks. If your notes form a pattern (such as 347, 34, 34 in a row), the page will highlight it and offer to clean up your notes.
  • Digits turn grey on the pad once all nine are placed. Tap a digit with no empty cell selected to highlight it everywhere.
