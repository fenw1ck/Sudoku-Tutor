Sudoku Tutor

Pick a level, solve, and ask for a hint whenever you’re stuck. Each hint names the technique and shows how it works.

Technique guide

Level 1. Full House: a unit with one empty cell. Last Digit: a digit placed 8 times has a forced 9th spot. Scanning (Cross-Hatching): the digit’s rows and columns block all but one cell of a box.

Level 2. Naked Single: a cell with one candidate. Hidden Single: a digit with only one possible cell in a row, column or box.

Level 3. Naked Pair: two cells share the same two candidates, so those digits leave the rest of the unit. Hidden Pair: two digits fit only the same two cells, so those cells lose their other candidates.

Level 4. Pointing Pair/Triple (Locked Candidates 1): in a box, a digit lines up in one row or column, so it leaves the rest of that line. Box/Line Reduction, or Claiming (Locked Candidates 2): in a line, a digit sits in one box, so it leaves the rest of that box.

Level 5. X-Wing: a digit has two spots in each of two lines, and they align on the same two crossing lines. It leaves the rest of those crossing lines.

Level 6. XY-Wing: a pivot {A,B} sees wings {A,C} and {B,C}. Cells that see both wings can’t be C.

Levels 7 and 8 (chains, colouring, uniqueness techniques and Almost Locked Sets) aren’t generated here yet.
